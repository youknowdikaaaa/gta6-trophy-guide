Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no online play needed, roughly 200–250 hours. Story missions can be replayed, so mission trophies cannot be missed — the only trophy guides flag as possibly missable is Positive Mental Attitude.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story. Chapters 1–4 and the finale. Story trophies (13) pop as you finish chapters and named missions. Take the optional Chapter 4 mission that opens bank vaults.
  2. Stage 2 — Strangers, as they open. Finish each stranger strand in the chapter it appears (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone). Strands can lock as the story moves on — do not leave them for the post-game. Eight strands earn the silver.
  3. Stage 3 — Meditate early. Hit every meditation spot as you explore (18). This is the one possibly missable trophy — clear each spot the first time you pass it.
  4. Stage 4 — Activities and skills. Shooting ranges (42 golds), stunts (250, keep going to 300 for 100%), races, drone races, fishing, arcade, hunting, bowling and the rest. Try all 9 activity types once for Master of None.
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles and cleanup. Cards (3 of 18 sets), WAiNK It (100), vehicles (100), garages (8), all 52 areas, challenges (50, then 70 for 100%), bank vaults (5, then all 8 for 100%), then the 100% checklist for A Long Road — and the platinum.
